How we'll work

The standards we're opening with.

Most people hire a lawyer a handful of times in their life, usually under stress. The firm is being set up so that the experience is legible: you should always know what stage your matter is at, what it has cost so far, and what happens next.

These commitments will be written into our engagement letters, not just this website.

  • Fees quoted before work begins Flat fees where the matter allows it; written estimates where it doesn't.
  • Your file is yours Copies of everything filed or received, provided as the matter proceeds.
  • Plain-language memos Every significant decision point summarized in a page you can share with your family or partners.
  • No surprise withdrawals If we can't take or keep your matter, we say so early and explain why.
