Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

This policy covers the pre-launch website at mahmoodislawfirm.com. It is deliberately short because, right now, this site does very little with your information.

What we collect

The site has no analytics, no advertising trackers, and sets no cookies of its own. The waitlist form on this site does not transmit anything: until the firm launches, what you type stays in your browser and is discarded when you leave the page.

Fonts

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google's servers. Google may log standard request data such as your IP address. See Google's own privacy policy for how it handles those requests.

Hosting logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, timestamps) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

No attorney-client information

Please do not send confidential details about any legal matter through this site. Information submitted before the firm opens is not protected by attorney-client privilege and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

When the firm opens

At launch this policy will be replaced with one describing how the operating firm handles client inquiries, intake information, and communications. The replacement will be posted at this address with a new date.

Questions

Contact details for privacy questions will be published here when the firm opens.
